Professional Summary

Postdoctoral fellow at the Chaire C2M, Télécom Paris, working on surrogate modeling for the spatial and temporal prediction of RF-EMF exposure to telecommunications networks. Ph.D. in electrical engineering (GeePs, Université Paris-Saclay / Politecnico di Torino) on metamodel-based methods for human exposure assessment around high-power systems, followed by a postdoc at LATMOS on the EFIELD/Dragonfly experiment to Titan.
