I am a postdoctoral fellow in the [Chaire C2M](https://chairec2m.wp.imt.fr/en/accueil-english/) at [Télécom Paris](https://www.telecom-paris.fr/), Institut Polytechnique de Paris, working with Shanshan Wang and Joe Wiart on spatial and temporal prediction of RF-EMF exposure to telecommunications networks using surrogate modeling. Prior to that, I was a postdoctoral fellow at [LATMOS](https://latmos.ipsl.fr/), Sorbonne Université, from 2023 to 2025, working with Alice Le Gall on the scientific return of the EFIELD electric-field sensor on board NASA's Dragonfly mission to Titan. I received my Ph.D. in electrical engineering in 2023 from [GeePs](https://www.geeps.centralesupelec.fr/index.php?page=home), Université Paris-Saclay, in a joint program with [Politecnico di Torino](https://www.denerg.polito.it/en/), supervised by Lionel Pichon and Fabio Freschi, on metamodel-based methods for the fast prediction of human exposure to electromagnetic fields around high-power systems.

## Research interests

- Surrogate modeling and uncertainty quantification for electromagnetic problems
- Assessment of Human Exposure to Electromagnetic Fields
- Computational electromagnetics for planetary-science instrumentation
